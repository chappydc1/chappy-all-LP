Overview of Shilajit

Shilajit is a natural substance that has been revered for centuries in traditional medicine, particularly in Ayurvedic practices. It is a sticky, tar-like resin that oozes from rocks in high mountain ranges like the Himalayas. Known for its purported health benefits, Shilajit is rich in fulvic acid and a wide range of minerals, which are believed to contribute to its restorative properties.
